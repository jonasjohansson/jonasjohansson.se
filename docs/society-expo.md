# Society Expo — project sources and media

Prepared 9 September 2026 for `/society-expo/`. Jonas confirmed in this conversation that he did the lighting and Rose Hallgren built the cloud. He later supplied the tree installation's fabrication details: the trees were cut in half, left to dry for a few months, then drilled with a channel for an LED cable. The installation used WS2815 LEDs at 96 LEDs per metre, QuinLED controllers and custom software. These details come directly from Jonas; no controller model, software architecture or wider design responsibilities have been assumed.

## Exhibition research

Society Expo Exhibition is at Skellefteå museum on Nordanå, with published dates of **27 May 2026–24 January 2027**. It is produced by Skellefteå kommun and Try New Things in collaboration with the museum. Its four themes connect nature and local history with innovation and the future. The museum describes environments that respond to visitors' movement and a particular focus on children and young people. [Museum exhibition listing](https://skellefteamuseum.se/utstallning/society-expo-exhibition-2/).

The municipal visitor page names the four parts as nature's energy, human energy, society's energy and future energy. Sound, light, projections and participation connect the story, including Finnfors hydropower, Sara kulturhus and the upheaval around Northvolt. [Municipal exhibition overview](https://skelleftea.se/society-expo-2026/society-expo-2026/event--program/besok-se26-exhibition).

The visitor booklet explains the lighting's narrative role: a river leads through a recreation of the Finnfors tunnel; the resulting light continues through the exhibition as a symbol of energy. The final room brings together climate and environment, housing, mobility, skills supply and social sustainability. [Accessible exhibition booklet, especially pages 3 and 9–10](https://skelleftea.se/download/18.730a94c419e632960b613f3/1779873541499/SE26Exhibition_Broschyr_tillg%C3%A4nglighetsanpassad.pdf).

Work began with early conversations in late 2023. A preliminary study involved around 60 people, including secondary-school students. Recorded voices, shared opinions and environments that react to visitors make participation part of the experience. Linus Johansson is identified as Try New Things' Creative Experience Director. [Municipality's development interview](https://skelleftea.se/society-expo-2026/en/society-expo-2026/participation/projects/projects/society-expo-exhibition).

During the May preview, families helped colour the large mural. The team also observed how young visitors used the installations ahead of opening. [Municipal preview report](https://skelleftea.se/society-expo-2026/society-expo-2026/nyheter/2026-05-15-fullt-hus-nar-barn-och-unga-fick-fortur-till-society-expo-exhibition).

Try New Things describes the project as an exhibition with more than 50 interactive installations. The portfolio copy leaves out promotional superlatives and the installation count because they are unnecessary to explain the lighting. [Studio project overview](https://trynewthings.fail/experiencedesign/).

The wider Society Expo took place **27 May–9 June 2026**, distinct from the museum exhibition's longer run. [Society Expo FAQ](https://skelleftea.se/society-expo-2026/en/society-expo-2026/about-se26/faq).

Try New Things' [press room](https://trynewthings.fail/press/) links to [the Society Expo media page](https://trynewthings.fail/press/society-expo-2026/) and [@trynewthings.fail](https://instagram.com/trynewthings.fail). The press page was read directly to verify its image credits. Instagram pages for the studio and [@societyexpo2026](https://www.instagram.com/societyexpo2026/) could not be retrieved, so no claims are attributed to unseen Instagram posts.

`lighting.mp4` is a silent 8.88-second edit (222 frames), at 1920 × 1080 and 25 fps. At Jonas's request, the opening uses only unoccupied exhibition spaces, the lighting and the cloud. It is assembled from these supplied clips in order, ending before each following shot:

1. `2_Complexity_16x9_NO_Text.mp4`: 0–3.04 seconds (76 frames).
2. `IndividualAction_16x9_noText.mp4`: 16.6–18.68 seconds (52 frames).
3. `Full exhibition_v4 Master.mp4`: 71.72–73.6 seconds (47 frames).
4. `Full exhibition_v4 Master.mp4`: 82.64–84.52 seconds (47 frames).

FFmpeg scales and encodes the segments with H.264, CRF 21, `yuv420p`, then concatenates them with `+faststart`. `lighting-poster.jpg` is its exact first decoded frame; the existing site pipeline also uses it for the project strip and social preview.

The revised opening was checked across all 222 frames using contact sheets, including every cut boundary; no visitors appear. The previous 12.4-second edit is retained locally at `.cache/society-expo/lighting-original-edit.mp4`. The production build and focused browser checks pass with the revised 8.88-second duration.

`exhibition-film.mp4` retains the full 90.12-second supplied `Full exhibition_v4 Master.mp4`, compressed to 1920 × 1080 H.264 at CRF 25, a 2400 kb/s maximum video rate and 4800 kb buffer, with 128 kb/s AAC audio and `+faststart`. Its poster is taken at four seconds to avoid the source's black opening. Body video controls expose playback and sound; the opening film follows the site's existing muted preview and reduced-motion behaviour.

All media has authored image alt text or a video accessible label. The new project uses the existing project schema, responsive image pipeline, navigation, metadata and print layout.

## Validation

`npm test` passes all five existing tests and `npm run build` succeeds. Focused Chromium checks cover 1440 × 900, 390 × 844 and 844 × 390: no horizontal overflow, all remaining images load, both photograph pairs align on desktop, credits and canonical metadata are present, and reduced motion prevents automatic film downloads. The tree and greenhouse images span the content width as standalone figures. Homepage navigation opens the new page, both films play with the expected durations, the opening film pauses offscreen, and return navigation works. Direct content and the body film's controls also render without JavaScript. The generated sitemap includes the route and all local `src` and `poster` paths resolve.

Desktop and mobile screenshots were visually reviewed. Captures and check results are retained in the ignored `screenshots/society-expo/` folder. Video payloads total 32,771,391 bytes; the opening edit is 4,408,499 bytes and the full film is 28,362,892 bytes. No shared template, stylesheet or JavaScript changes were needed.
