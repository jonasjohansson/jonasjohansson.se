Jonas Johansson is a multidisciplinary artist and designer working at the intersection of technology, light, and interactive experiences. His practice explores the relationship between digital and physical spaces through innovative installations and immersive environments.

With a background in both traditional art forms and cutting-edge technology, Jonas creates works that challenge our perception of space and time, often incorporating elements of sound, light, and user interaction.

His work spans across multiple disciplines including light installations, interactive media, stage design, and educational projects. Each piece is carefully crafted to create meaningful connections between the audience and the technological environment.

Jonas believes in the power of "Progress Not Perfection" - a philosophy that drives his experimental approach to art and technology, always pushing boundaries while maintaining accessibility and human connection.
