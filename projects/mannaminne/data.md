      Mannaminne is made of fleeting film sequences I generated on AI
      excursions into Hilma af Klint's symbolic world, Tomas Tranströmer's
      words and our digital memory. It starts from the first line of
      Tranströmer's poem Gläntan: deep in the forest there is an unexpected
      clearing that can only be found by someone who has got lost. Working
      with AI felt much like wandering curiously through a forest, with
      uncertainty for company. Everything begins with a prompt: a word, a
      sentence or an image.

      The room is Superellipsen, a glass-walled space at Plattan, below the
      Sergel fountain. Visitors walked between the scrims and cast their
      shadows into the images. The
      work grew out of Gläntan, a smaller piece for Nonagon earlier in 2021:
      the same Tranströmer line as a generative audiovisual poem projected
      between two walls, with generative music by Away From People.

      Mannaminne opened in Superellipsen in November 2021. Stockholms stad,
      through Norrmalms stadsdelsförvaltning, then commissioned me to extend
      and develop the work through December, as part of På Sergels torg,
      and over the darkest days of the year the room turned into Noneon.

  - type: text

    content: >-
      Noneon is a fluorescent creature with a vivid imagination, lightning
      hair and a fondness for the spectrum of light, whose dreams I brought
      to life in the room. Six hanging LED signs that look like neon carried
      winding, branching patterns grown from reaction-diffusion. From
      December to mid-January the empty dark room became a workshop, a
      stage and a place for reflection and interaction, with new sound and light
      works made along the way.
