A learning resource covering creative technology, from TouchDesigner and GLSL shaders to Arduino and machine learning. Used as course material for workshops at Hyper Island and Beckmans. It also documents Exploring Technology Live, a 15-session Twitch series organized during COVID in 2020 with guest instructors covering generative design, A-Frame, Hydra, SuperCollider, and more.
