A learning resource I put together covering creative technology, from TouchDesigner and GLSL shaders to Arduino and machine learning. I used it as course material for workshops at Hyper Island and Beckmans. It also documents Exploring Technology Live, a 15-session Twitch series I organized during COVID in 2020 with guest instructors covering generative design, A-Frame, Hydra, SuperCollider, and more.
