      An early exploration into the relationship between light and space, Lyra creates a constellation of suspended light sources that respond to environmental conditions and human presence.
  - type: text
    content: >-
      The installation uses custom sensors to detect changes in air movement, temperature, and proximity, translating these invisible forces into dynamic light patterns that shift and evolve throughout the day.
  - type: text
    content: >-
      Named after the constellation, the work invites viewers to contemplate the vastness of space and our place within it, using light as a medium to connect the cosmic with the intimate.
