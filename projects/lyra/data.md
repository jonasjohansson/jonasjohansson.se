  - type: text

    content: >-
      Lyra was my first foray into interactive light art, made while I was
      working at Moment Factory in Montreal and shown at C2 Montreal. A set of
      fishing wire strings was strung tightly from floor to ceiling, each fitted
      with an accelerometer. When a visitor plucked a string, the sensor fed its
      peak value into an algorithm simulating a vibrating chord. Light and sound
      grew from the tip of the string and travelled along the walls and ceiling,
      turning the room into a kind of circuitry. The more people played, the
      richer it got. Built on Arduino.
