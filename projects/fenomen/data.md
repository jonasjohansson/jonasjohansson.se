  - type: text

    content: >-
      Fenomen is a concert by four musicians from Halland: Tomas Larsson on
      guitar, Mattias Grönroos on piano and bass, Fia Forslund on vibraphone
      and Michael Edlund on drums. Each piece starts from a phenomenon, from
      northern lights and snowflakes to lightning and bioluminescence, and the
      music moves between composed parts and improvisation in step with the
      images.

    content: >-
      Tomas and I designed the stage: a black gauze in front of the band and
      screens behind it, so the projection lands on both, with the musicians
      standing in between. I made the moving image and the lighting. Part of
      the material came from a desk in the stalls, where colour and liquids on
      a light table were filmed from above, next to a microscope.
