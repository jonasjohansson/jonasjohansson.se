A head-mounted projector setup that alters what you see in real time. A webcam captures the scene, Depth Anything v3 generates a depth map, and ControlNet steers Stable Diffusion img2img to transform the image before it gets projected back. Keyboard shortcuts for changing prompts and intensity on the fly. Built for an RTX 4090.
