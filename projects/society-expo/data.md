  - type: text

    content: >-
      I did the lighting for Society Expo Exhibition at [Skellefteå
      museum](https://skellefteamuseum.se/utstallning/society-expo-exhibition-2/),
      with [Try New Things](https://trynewthings.fail/). Part of Society Expo
      2026, it tells the story of the city's transformation in four parts,
      following the energy of nature, people, society and the future.
  - type: image

  - type: text
    fontSize: small
    content: >-
      The first room is forest, rock and water, and here the light runs
      through real tree trunks. We cut the trees in half and left them to dry
      for a few months, then drilled a channel and fitted the LED inside:
      WS2815 at 96 LEDs per metre, on QuinLED controllers with custom
      software. From there visitors follow the river through a recreation of
      the Finnfors hydropower tunnel, where water becomes electricity.
