    content: >-
      Lumia premiered at Dramalogen in Halmstad on 23 September 2022, then
      played Forum in Hyltebruk on 30 September and Teaterklubben at Halmstad
      Teater during Kulturnatten on 1 October. Each of the seven pieces had its
      own visual idea. I built generators the band could play: rain that the
      vibraphone and drums set falling in the intro, patterns that followed
      the trumpet's volume, and lines and waves that traced Per Thornberg's
      saxophone, each note shifting the colour. Gamla stan ran 1950s and 60s
      footage from 8 mm Halmstad by
      Stefan Petzén and Johan Rosenlind under sound-reactive filters.

    content: >-
      For Oket, a story about a farmer whose ox dies, and The Bolmen Express,
      about the old steam railway from Halmstad to Bolmen, I generated AI
      animations styled after Josef Frank and John Bauer. In Oket the guitar
      set how fast the animation played.

  - type: text

    content: >-
      The band was Tomas Larsson on guitar, Malte Zeberg on double bass, Per
      Thornberg on saxophone, Adam Sass on trumpet, Fia Forslund on
      vibraphone, Jonathan Kabell on drums and Axel Croné on clarinet. Anton
      Wernebäck ran sound, Charlotte Ek Wirack produced, Klara Viridén made
      the graphics and Servando Barreiro did programming. Lumia was a project
      by Tomas Larsson with support from Region Halland, Kulturrådet and Hylte
      Kommun, in collaboration with Dramalogen. In 2023 I made the visuals for
      Lumia II as well, at Festaurang in Halmstad. Concert photos by [Rose
      Hallgren](https://rosehallgren.se/).
