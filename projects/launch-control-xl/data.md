A browser-based replica of the Novation Launch Control XL using the Web MIDI API. Three rows of knobs, eight faders, two rows of buttons, all sending real MIDI. I made it so any touchscreen or laptop can work as a control surface without the physical hardware. Labels are editable and saved between sessions.
