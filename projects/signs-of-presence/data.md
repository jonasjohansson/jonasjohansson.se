      In February 2026, [People in Orbit](https://peopleinorbit.se) performed
      Signs of Presence at Barnens Scen in Malmö, a reinterpretation of the
      [graphic score](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graphic_notation_(music))
      created by electronic pioneer Leo Nilsson and artist Al Eklund in 1978.
      The original sheets were photographed, cleaned up and redrawn in white
      on black for projection, with their expression markings set in type.

    content: >-
      Then I turned the idea around. While the band interpreted the notations,
      I drew a new score out of what I heard, live on a tablet in a
      [custom web app](https://jonasjohansson.github.io/signsofpresence-app/)
      I built for it, and composed the projection in
      [Resolume](https://resolume.com/) with custom FFGL shaders.

      The performance became the pre-study for the
      [Signs of Presence Tour](https://signsofpresence.com), a Nordic tour of
      existing graphic scores that Adam Sass and I are planning for Stockholm,
      Helsinki, Reykjavík and Copenhagen in autumn 2027, playing scores from
      the 1960s to 1980s with a local musician in each city. We applied for
      project funding in September 2026. I also make the band's
      [album artwork](/people-in-orbit/).
