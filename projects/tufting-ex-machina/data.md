  - type: image
    src: 02.jpg
  - type: text
    fontSize: small
    content: >-
      Tufting Ex Machina was a two-day workshop at
      [Aavistus](https://www.aavistusfestival.fi/), the audiovisual festival in
      Helsinki I have been part of since it started in 2022, as an artist and
      through [Nordic Audiovisual Artists](https://www.nava.community). Each of
      the ten participants went looking for their cultural, national or
      personal identity in patterns, then used sketching and generative AI to
      combine and remix them into something new. I'd learned tufting during a
      residency at
      [Konvent Zero](https://konventzero.com/) in Spain.
  - type: image
    src: 03.jpg
  - type: text
    fontSize: small
    content: >-
      The method was the
      [Surrealists'](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surrealism)
      [exquisite corpse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exquisite_corpse),
      where each person adds to a picture without seeing the whole of it,
      responding only to what came before.
  - type: image
    src: 04.jpg
  - type: text
    fontSize: small
    content: >-
      The participants worked in groups, each designing and tufting a square
      and then passing it on for the next group to respond to, picking up each
      other's shapes, colours and patterns. It ended as 24 tufted squares
      covering 140 x 210 cm.
