  - type: text
    content: >-
      Lars Lerin's paintings, voice and archives surround the audience in
      [Det Mörka Ljuset](https://detmorkaljuset.se/), a film by Sara Broos
      made for the walls and floor of [Elverket](https://elverket.com/)
      in Stockholm.
  - type: text
    fontSize: small
    content: >-
      The film follows Lars through his paintings, home movies, photographs
      and his own telling of his life. Sara Broos developed the concept,
      directed and edited it. The main hall at Elverket is roughly 34 metres
      long, eleven metres wide and eight metres high, with images on all four
      walls and on the floor. A composition can surround you, but you can
      only look at part of it at once.
  - type: text
    fontSize: small
    content: >-
      I made animation for the film. Much of the work was about how one
      painting moves into another: spreading pigment, wet edges, blooms,
      ripples, light and mist, always starting from the paintings
      themselves. I also worked on flocks of birds moving through the hall and
      built a browser model of the room so we could see how a house, a
      face or a stretch of water would land on the walls and floor while
      the images were still being made.
