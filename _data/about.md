Jonas Johansson <span id="age-placeholder">(1987–)</span> is an anywhere-based artist, educator, and designer from Glommen, Halland, working with light ✺ technology through ❋ play, community, and (un)human intervention.

He lectured in Visual Communication at [Beckmans](https://beckmans.se) (2017–2025). Since 2024 he has designed courses for Svenska Tecknare. He has been Industry Leader at Hyper Island (2014–2022) and taught at Berghs, Nyckelviken, and Futuregames. He has served as an Assessor for Kulturbryggan under the Swedish Arts Grants Committee. Earlier roles include technology-driven design at KRAM/WEISSHAAR (Stockholm/Munich), digital object design at [FIELD](https://field.io) (London), and immersive experience design at [Moment Factory](https://momentfactory.com) (Montreal).

He runs the artist residency [Visualia](https://visualia.se), is a member of the Stockholm-based lighting collective [Svartljus](https://svartljus.se), and co-founded [Nordic Audiovisual Artists](https://nava.community). He shares a studio in Södermalm with [SMASH](https://smash.studio), [IVAR](https://ivar.studio), and [Rose Hallgren](https://rosehallgren.se).
