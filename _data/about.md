I’m Jonas Johansson <span id="age-placeholder"></span>, an artist, educator, and designer from Glommen, Halland. My work explores light and technology through play, community, and (un)human intervention.

As an artist and designer, I create installations, products, experiences, sculptures, and games. My approach is ludic and exploratory, often hybrid and interdisciplinary, moving between art, technology, and collective experimentation.

Over eight years were spent teaching Visual Communication at [Beckmans](https://beckmans.se) (2017–2025), followed by designing courses for Svenska Tecknare since 2024. Roles have also included Industry Leader at Hyper Island (2014–2022) and teaching positions at Aalto University, Berghs School of Communication, Nyckelviken, and Futuregames. Alongside this, I hosted the first [Processing Community Day](https://processing.org/) in Stockholm and established [Exploring Technology](https://www.exploring.technology), an open-source repository of tools, methods, and resources from workshops and courses.

Professional practice includes technology-driven design at KRAM/WEISSHAAR (SE/DE), digital objects at [FIELD](https://field.io) (UK), and immersive experiences at [Moment Factory](https://momentfactory.com) (CA). Before Montreal, internships were completed at [W+K Tokyo](https://www.wktokyo.com/en/) and [UltraSuperNew](https://ultrasupernew.com/) in Japan. I've also worked as an Assessor for Kulturbryggan under the Swedish Arts Grants Committee.

Today the focus lies in running the artist residency [Visualia](https://visualia.se), collaborating with the lighting collective [Svartljus](https://svartljus.se), and co-founding [Nordic Audiovisual Artists](https://nava.community). A studio is shared in Södermalm with [SMASH](https://smash.studio), [IVAR](https://ivar.studio), and [Rose Hallgren](https://rosehallgren.se).
